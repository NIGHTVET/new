Most Asked Questions

What devices can I use?

Nightwave TV supports common streaming platforms including Fire TV/Firestick, Android TV/Google TV, Android mobile devices and Apple devices. Compatibility can vary by setup.

Can I use more than one device?

Yes. Monthly and yearly plans offer multiple-device options, and the lifetime option includes unlimited devices.

How do I upgrade or change my plan?

Contact Nightwave TV support with your current account information and the plan you would like to move to.

What happens after I submit an order?

Your order request is delivered to Nightwave TV. Support can then follow up with you to complete the next steps and device setup.
